# For my German tutors

I'm learning German from scratch, aiming for everyday conversation. My native language is Spanish and I speak English fluently. Our lessons are the most valuable part of my practice, and a few things would make them even more useful:

1. **Correct me every time I make a mistake**, even small ones like the wrong article or verb ending.
2. **Give me a moment to fix it myself first.** Point out where the mistake is ("Noch mal: *gestern …*"), then tell me the correct form if I can't find it.
3. **Say the correct version clearly once.** I review my lessons afterwards, and a clear correction is easy to find.
4. **Speak slowly and keep to simple German**, switching to English only when I'm stuck.
5. **At the end, name the two or three things I should practise before next time.**

I'd like to record our lessons with Granola, a note-taking app, so I can review them afterwards. It makes a transcript that only I can see, and I use it to make my own flashcards. Is that OK with you? If you'd rather I didn't, just say so and I won't.

Danke!
